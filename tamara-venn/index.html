04 — Art

Tamara Venn

Vibrant, richly coloured paintings of Cambodia — its wildlife, its streets, and the people working to protect them.

Tamara Venn’s paintings are inspired by Cambodia, the natural world and the people living alongside it and working to protect it.

Hornbills over city rooftops, gibbons in the forest, plastic chairs on patterned tiles: her work celebrates nature and colour in the Kingdom of Wonder.

From original paintings to prints and postcards, everyone can take home a piece of this beautiful country.

Medium
Original paintings, prints, postcards
Subjects
Wildlife, landscapes, city life
Commitment
Nature and its protection

Paintings

Wildlife and landscapes of Cambodia.

City life

Streets, shophouses and the everyday details of Cambodian cities.

Prints and cards

Prints and postcards, available at Atelier 464.

Find Tamara Venn at Atelier 464 — 31b, Street 464, Phnom Penh, Cambodia. Open every day.
