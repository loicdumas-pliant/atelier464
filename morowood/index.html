03 — Furniture & interiors

Morowood

Custom furniture and interiors that work — and last. Any size, made to measure, delivered to your home.

Morowood is a Siem Reap–based furniture workshop specialising in high-quality custom wood furniture and interior build-outs for homes, businesses and hospitality projects.

From bespoke kitchens and wardrobes to custom tables, shelving and complete fit-outs, Morowood combines European precision with skilled local craftsmanship to deliver made-to-measure solutions that fit your space and your budget.

The workshop uses renewable and repurposed wood, for responsible use of materials without compromising on strength or appearance.

Morowood stands behind what it builds: every piece comes with a 10-year warranty — a straightforward reflection of the quality of its materials and workmanship.
