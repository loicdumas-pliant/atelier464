01 — Footwear

Kingdom of Wow

Cosy handmade wool slippers and easy summer espadrilles — timeless, fun, and made with love.

Kingdom of Wow crafts footwear inspired by quality and comfort. Every pair is thoughtfully made by local women in Siem Reap, using natural materials.

The brand embraces slow-fashion values: designs that are timeless and fun rather than seasonal, made to be worn for years.

Kingdom of Wow is proudly B Corp Certified.

Made by
Local women artisans, Siem Reap
Collections
Wool slippers, summer espadrilles
Materials
Wool, leather and other natural materials
Certified
B Corp

Slippers

Hand-knitted wool uppers on leather soles, in colours from Glacier Teal to Mulberry.

Espadrilles

Light, easy espadrilles for warm days — for women and men.

The workshop

Every pair is made by hand by the Kingdom of Wow team in Siem Reap.
