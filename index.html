01 — Footwear

Kingdom of Wow

Cosy handmade wool slippers and easy summer espadrilles — timeless, fun, and made with love.

Every pair is thoughtfully crafted by local women in Siem Reap, using natural materials. Kingdom of Wow embraces slow fashion: designs that last well beyond a season.

02 — Fashion

Maison Sirivan

A French design sensibility for contemporary Cambodian fashion: clean silhouettes, natural fabrics, precise cuts.

Born in Phnom Penh and raised in Paris’s Sentier textile district, designer Sirivan Chak Dumas trained at Atelier Chardon-Savard before bringing her craft home in 2006. Since 2011, every garment has been made in her own atelier in Siem Reap — from the first sketch and pattern to the final stitch. Nothing is outsourced.

Linen and cotton chosen for the tropics, produced in small series, built to last rather than to follow trends.

Designer
Sirivan Chak Dumas
Materials
Linen and cotton
Atelier
In-house, Siem Reap — since 2011

04 — Art

Tamara Venn

Vibrant, richly coloured paintings of Cambodia — its wildlife, its streets, and the people working to protect them.

Tamara Venn’s work celebrates nature and colour in the Kingdom of Wonder. Original paintings, prints and postcards: everyone can take home a piece of this beautiful country.

Medium
Paintings, prints, postcards
Subjects
Wildlife, landscapes, city life
Commitment
Nature and its protection

The space

A department store for contemporary Cambodian design.

Fashion, art, footwear and interiors under one roof. Wander through a complete lifestyle space where each universe complements the others — come for a pair of slippers, leave with a painting, or the idea for a new kitchen.

The space also hosts small guest corners, where we invite creators with complementary expertise to join us for a while.

What we share

  • SlowSmall series and timeless pieces, never fast fashion.
  • EthicalEthically handmade in Cambodia by skilled artisans.
  • NaturalLinen, cotton, wool, renewable and repurposed wood.
  • DurableMade to last — up to a 10-year warranty on furniture.
