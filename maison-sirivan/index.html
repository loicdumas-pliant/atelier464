02 — Fashion

Maison Sirivan

A French design sensibility for contemporary Cambodian fashion: clean silhouettes, natural fabrics, precise cuts.

The driving force behind the label is Sirivan Chak Dumas. Born in Phnom Penh, she moved to France at the age of twelve and grew up in Paris’s historic Sentier textile district.

She trained at the renowned Atelier Chardon-Savard and sharpened her eye for detail working with master bootmaker Maurice Arnoult. Her return to professional life in Cambodia began in 2006, with a collection designed for Artisans d’Angkor.

Since 2011, Maison Sirivan has managed its entire production in-house. Nothing is outsourced: from the first sketch and pattern to cutting and the final stitch, every step happens in Sirivan’s own workshop in Siem Reap.

The brand deliberately stays away from fast fashion. It works in small series, with premium linen and cotton chosen for the tropics, and builds timeless pieces with serious attention to detail.
